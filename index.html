Frequently Asked

Common questions about our services.

What should I wear?

We provide comfortable loose-fitting clothing. Just come in comfortable underwear or swimwear that allows easy movement.

How long is each session?

Sessions range from 1 hour for regular massage to 2 hours for combo packages. Your therapist will inform you of the duration when you book.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes, we recommend booking 1-2 days ahead to secure your preferred time. Walk-ins are subject to availability.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please cancel at least 4 hours before your appointment. Late cancellations may affect your ability to book in the future.

Do you offer home service?

Yes! We offer home service within Marawi City. Select "Home Service" when booking, and our therapist will come to your location.

Is Thai Boran massage painful?

It should not be painful. You'll feel pressure and stretching, but communicate with your therapist if you need less pressure.
